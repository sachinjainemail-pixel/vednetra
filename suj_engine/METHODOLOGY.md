### 2.0 KNOWLEDGE BASE 1. DOMINANT HOUSE (STELLIUM) RULE

Added by the owner in the second instruction round. This runs FIRST in the D1 analysis, for every question,

OWNER'S RULE (own words, lightly cleaned):

In the D1 chart, always look for the house where many planets are together, two or three or four planets or
more in the same house. If that is the case, the native will have the biggest impact of that house and its
significations in his or her life. For example, if a native has three planets in the fourth house, the life
will carry the most impact from the fourth house significations.

What kind of impact it has is decided by the planets posited in it. The results of that house's significations
are delivered THROUGH the planets posited in it, by BOTH their natural significations AND their functional
significations based on the chart (the houses they lord for this ascendant).

WORKED EXAMPLE FROM THE OWNER (an illustration only, the rule is NOT limited to Saturn or to the fourth house):
Three planets sit in the fourth house and Saturn is one of them. Saturn is the natural significator of
employment, job, work and karma, so the native does mostly fourth house related activities during life, as per
Saturn's natural signification. That means work such as home tuitions or a homemaker kind of role. If the
native is also the functional lord of the ninth and tenth houses, the native will be doing the karmas or works
related to the father or the father's work, or the native's recognition will come with delay in life.

GENERALISATION (explicit instruction from the owner): the above applies to ANY planet in ANY house, and
especially to houses holding several planets. If Mars, Sun, Moon and Venus sit in one house, that house carries
the natural and functional significations of ALL four planets, the native's life revolves around that house's
significations, and the results of those significations are delivered through those four planets. Always
analyse the chart by asking which house has the most planets, what that house signifies, and what the natural
and functional significations of each planet inside it are.

HOW THE ENGINE APPLIES IT (operational steps):

1. Count planets by house in the D1 chart. A stellium is any house with two or more planets. The house (or
   houses) with the highest count is the DOMINANT house. A house with fewer planets but still two or more is a
   secondary stellium house and is also reported.
2. For each planet in a stellium house, list (a) its natural significations, (b) its functional significations,
   meaning the houses it lords and what those houses signify, (c) its functional role for this ascendant,
   (d) its condition, which means dignity, retrogression, combustion, degree, nakshatra and pada, and
   Navamsha placement. Condition decides whether the delivery is strengthened or damaged, using the
   positive first then discount approach from section 1.
3. Combine house significations with planet significations. The house gives the AREA of life and the planets
   give the MODE and the QUALITY of delivery. Write this as a plain life theme statement, as in the owner's
   example, for every planet in the house.
4. Carry the dominant house into the question. If the question's house is the dominant house, or the dominant
   house planets lord the question's house, or the question's house lord sits in the dominant house, the finding
   is a major weight for the promise. Otherwise the dominant house still colours how the event will be
   experienced and must be stated.
5. Check the same finding in the Moon chart and the Sun chart (Sudarshan). A dominant house that holds in more
   than one chart is a stronger finding.
6. The finding must be reflected in the closing summary section of the answer.

b. If two houses tie for the highest count, both are dominant and ranked for display only.

d. Houses are whole sign houses counted from the reference sign, matching Vednetra's house field.

## 4. DASHA ANALYSIS: HOW DASHA RESULTS TO BE INTERPRETED

In Vedic astrology, Dasha is the timing system that tells when the promise of the horoscope will fructify.
The natal chart shows what is promised; dasha shows when and how strongly it manifests.
The most commonly used system is Vimshottari Dasha, based on the Moon's nakshatra at birth.

### 4.1 Fundamental Rule

A planet gives results according to:

1. Its natural nature
   Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu are natural malefics.
   Jupiter, Venus, well-placed Mercury, bright Moon are natural benefics.
2. Its functional nature
   This depends on ascendant.
   Example: For Taurus Lagna, Saturn rules 9th and 10th houses, so Saturn becomes a powerful yogakaraka.
   But for Cancer Lagna, Saturn rules 7th and 8th, so it can give struggle, delay, separation, disease, or
   transformation.
3. Houses owned by the planet
   A planet gives results of the houses it owns.
   Example: If Mars rules 4th and 9th for Leo Lagna, Mars dasha can give property, vehicle, father's support,
   luck, higher education.
4. House occupied by the planet
   A planet strongly gives results of the house where it sits.
   Example: 10th lord sitting in 12th: career linked to foreign lands, hospitals, isolation, MNC, remote work,
   or loss/change in career depending on dignity.
5. Planets conjunct it
   Conjunction modifies results.
   Example: Venus with Rahu can give luxury, foreign relationships, glamour, unconventional marriage, desire,
   media, fashion, or scandals.
6. Planets aspecting it
   Aspects activate or modify the dasha planet.
   Example: Jupiter aspecting 7th lord during Venus dasha can support marriage.
   Saturn aspecting 7th lord can delay marriage or create coldness.
7. Nakshatra lord
   A planet gives results of the nakshatra lord in which it is placed.
   Example: Venus sitting in Saturn's nakshatra will give Venus + Saturn results.
   So Venus dasha may give marriage/luxury, but with Saturn's delay, responsibility, age gap, duty, karma, or
   separation themes.
8. Divisional chart placement
   The planet must be checked in relevant varga.
   For marriage: D9/Navamsha.
   For career: D10/Dashamsha.
   For children: D7/Saptamsha.
   For education: D24/Siddhamsha.
   For parents: D12/Dwadashamsha.
   For disease/misfortune: D30/Trishamsha.

### 4.2 Dasha Works Only Within Natal Promise

Dasha cannot give something that is not promised in the chart.
If the chart has weak marriage promise, even Venus dasha may not give happy marriage.
If the chart has strong business promise, even Saturn dasha may give business expansion if Saturn connects to
7th, 10th, 11th, or 2nd houses.

Example. Suppose:
- 7th house is afflicted.
- 7th lord is combust.
- Venus is weak.
- D9 7th house is afflicted.
- Dasha of Venus runs.
Result: Marriage may happen, but with delay, dissatisfaction, separation, or troubled relationship.

But if:
- 7th lord is strong.
- Venus is strong.
- D9 is supportive.
- Dasha of 7th lord or Venus runs.
Result: Marriage is likely and stable.

### 4.3 Mahadasha, Antardasha, Pratyantardasha

Mahadasha gives the broad life theme.
Example: Saturn Mahadasha may bring responsibility, discipline, karma, delay, hard work, maturity, profession,
land, old people, servants, masses, chronic issues.

Antardasha gives the actual event trigger.
Example: During Saturn Mahadasha, Venus Antardasha may bring marriage if Venus connects to 7th house.

Pratyantardasha gives the precise event window.
Example: Saturn Mahadasha - Venus Antardasha - Moon Pratyantardasha may give marriage if Moon is 7th lord or
connected to Venus/7th house.

### 4.4 General Formula for Dasha Interpretation

For any dasha planet, judge:

1. What houses does it own?
2. Where is it placed?
3. Is it exalted, debilitated, own sign, friendly sign, enemy sign?
4. Is it retrograde or combust?
5. Is it in benefic or malefic conjunction?
6. Who aspects it?
7. Which nakshatra is it in?
8. What is its condition in relevant divisional chart?
9. Is it forming yoga?
10. Is current transit supporting the dasha promise?

(The owner's text on transit validation of SAT, JUP, RAH, KET and SUN during the selected dasha periods
appears in section 0 pillar c. Detailed transit rules are expected in a later instruction round.)

### 5.2 The strength method (owner's text, in Hindi, exactly as given)

ग्रहों की तुलना में पहले जन्मकुंडली का सामान्य ग्रहबल देखें। फिर जिस विषय का फल जानना है, उसमें उस ग्रह की भूमिका और सक्रियता देखें। कुंडली का सर्वाधिक बलवान ग्रह और करियर, विवाह या संतान के लिए सबसे प्रभावी ग्रह अलग हो सकते हैं।

1. षड्बल प्रतिशत। सात ग्रहों की सामान्य तुलना का पहला आधार षड्बल है। इसके छह घटक हैं स्थानबल, दिग्बल, कालबल, चेष्टाबल, नैसर्गिक बल और दृग्बल। हर ग्रह का प्राप्त षड्बल उसके अपने निर्धारित न्यूनतम से भाग देकर 100 से गुणा करें। आर. संथानम् के बृहत् पाराशर होरा शास्त्र संस्करण में न्यूनतम बल सूर्य के लिए 6.5, चंद्र के लिए 6, मंगल के लिए 5, बुध के लिए 7, गुरु के लिए 6.5, शुक्र के लिए 5.5 और शनि के लिए 5 रूप है। उदाहरण के लिए, बुध के 7.2 रूप लगभग 103% हैं, जबकि शनि के 6 रूप 120% हैं। इसलिए कुल रूप कम होने पर भी शनि अपने न्यूनतम के सापेक्ष ऊपर है। 100% का अर्थ निर्धारित न्यूनतम की पूर्ति है। छह घटकों को अलग देखने से पता चलता है कि ग्रह की बढ़त किस प्रकार के बल से आई है।

2. D1 में राशि और भाव की स्थिति। देखें कि ग्रह उच्च, मूलत्रिकोण, स्वराशि, मित्र राशि, शत्रु राशि या नीच राशि में है। साथ में उसके राशि-स्वामी की स्थिति और, जहाँ लागू हो, नीचभंग देखें। राशि की गरिमा ग्रह की स्थिति बताती है, जबकि भाव, भावेशत्व और योग बताते हैं कि उसका प्रभाव किन विषयों पर पड़ेगा।

3. D9 और विषय-संबंधी वर्ग। नवांश में ग्रह की गरिमा देखें। ग्रह वर्गोत्तम है तो जिस राशि में वह D1 और D9 दोनों में है, उसकी गुणवत्ता भी देखें। विंशोपक बल कई वर्गों में ग्रह की स्थिति का 20-अंकीय आकलन देता है। किसी खास विषय के लिए संबंधित वर्ग में ग्रह और भाव का संबंध भी देखें, जैसे करियर के लिए D10, संतान के लिए D7 और शिक्षा के लिए D24।

4. वक्री, अस्त और ग्रहयुद्ध। हर ग्रह की वक्री और अस्त स्थिति अलग दर्ज करें। वक्रता का असर चेष्टाबल में देखें, अस्तता के लिए सूर्य से वास्तविक अंश-दूरी देखें, और जहाँ ग्रहयुद्ध लागू हो वहाँ उसका परिणाम दर्ज करें। आपकी पद्धति में वक्री को अधिकतम और अस्त को शून्य मानने वाला समायोजन गणितीय षड्बल प्रतिशत से अलग रखें। इससे दोनों आधारों पर बनी रैंकिंग स्पष्ट रहेगी।

5. दृष्टि, युति और योग। देखें कि ग्रह को किन ग्रहों का समर्थन या पीड़ा मिल रही है, युति कितनी निकट है और ग्रह किस योग में भाग ले रहा है। दृग्बल षड्बल का एक घटक है, जबकि विशिष्ट युति या योग फल की प्रकृति समझने में मदद करता है। उच्चता, वर्ग-स्थिति, वक्रता और दृष्टि का जो हिस्सा षड्बल में पहले से शामिल है, उसे कुल स्कोर में दोबारा जोड़ने के बजाय स्कोर की व्याख्या में इस्तेमाल करें।

6. विषय से संबंध और दशा में सक्रियता। किसी खास फल के लिए लग्न और चंद्र से संबंधित भाव, भावेश और कारक देखें। फिर संबंधित वर्ग में पुष्टि और दशा–अंतरदशा में उनकी सक्रियता देखें। उदाहरण के लिए, करियर की रैंकिंग में दशम भाव और दशमेश से जुड़ा ग्रह, जिसकी भूमिका D10 में भी पुष्ट हो और जिसकी दशा सक्रिय हो, सामान्य षड्बल सूची में नीचे रहते हुए भी उस अवधि में अधिक प्रभावी हो सकता है।

राहु–केतु का आकलन अलग करें। पाराशरी षड्बल की यह गणना सूर्य से शनि तक सात ग्रहों के लिए है। राहु–केतु के लिए उनके राशि-स्वामी, भाव, युति, दृष्टि और दशा में सक्रियता देखें।

### 5.3 English rendering by the transcriber (for reference, the Hindi above is the authority)

First look at the general strength of the planets in the birth chart. Then look at the role and activity of
the planet in the subject whose result is wanted. The strongest planet of the chart and the most effective
planet for career, marriage or children can be different.

1. Shadbala percent. Shadbala is the first basis for comparing the seven planets. Its six parts are Sthana,
   Dig, Kala, Cheshta, Naisargika and Drik bala. Divide each planet's Shadbala by its own prescribed minimum and
   multiply by 100. In R. Santhanam's edition of Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra the minimum is Sun 6.5, Moon 6,
   Mars 5, Mercury 7, Jupiter 6.5, Venus 5.5 and Saturn 5 rupa. Example: Mercury with 7.2 rupa is about 103
   percent while Saturn with 6 rupa is 120 percent, so Saturn is higher relative to its minimum even though its
   total is lower. 100 percent means the minimum is met. Viewing the six parts separately shows what kind of
   strength gave the lead.
2. Sign and house condition in D1. Exalted, Moolatrikona, own, friendly, enemy or debilitated sign, the
   condition of the sign lord, and neecha bhanga where it applies. Sign dignity tells the planet's condition.
   House, lordship and yogas tell which subjects its influence falls on.
3. D9 and the subject varga. Dignity in Navamsha. If vargottama, also see the quality of the sign it holds in
   both D1 and D9. Vimshopaka bala is a 20 point assessment across many vargas. For a specific subject see the
   planet and house relation in the relevant varga, D10 for career, D7 for children, D24 for education.
4. Retrograde, combust and planetary war. Record each separately. See the effect of retrogression in Cheshta
   bala, use the actual degree distance from the Sun for combustion, and record the result of planetary war
   where it applies. The owner's adjustment that treats a retrograde planet as maximum and a combust planet as
   zero must be kept apart from the mathematical Shadbala percent, so both rankings stay visible.
5. Aspect, conjunction and yoga. See which planets support or afflict, how close the conjunction is, and
   which yoga the planet joins. Drik bala is already a part of Shadbala. A conjunction or yoga helps to
   understand the nature of the result. Whatever part of exaltation, varga position, retrogression and aspect is
   already inside Shadbala must be used to explain the score and must not be added again to the total.
6. Relation to the subject and activity in dasha. For a specific result see the houses, house lords and
   karakas from Lagna and from Moon, then confirmation in the relevant varga, then their activity in dasha and
   antardasha. Example: for career, a planet tied to the 10th house and 10th lord, confirmed in D10 and with its
   dasha active, can be more effective in that period even if it ranks low in the general Shadbala list.

Rahu and Ketu are assessed separately. The Parashari Shadbala calculation is for the seven planets Sun to
Saturn. For Rahu and Ketu look at their sign lord, house, conjunction, aspect and activity in dasha.

### 5.4 How the engine runs it

Tool: suj_engine/tools/planet_strength.js (data/topics.json holds the subject presets).
All figures come from Vednetra: Shadbala in virupas with the Santhanam minimums, Vimshopaka, dignity, aspects,
yogas, divisional charts and the dasha stack at the as-of date.

Provisional resolution procedure, built by the engine author from the six steps above. It awaits the owner's
confirmation or correction:
1. Each candidate planet is compared on four steps. Step 1 Shadbala percent. Step 2 D1 sign dignity, where a
   debilitation with neecha bhanga counts as a neutral sign. Step 3 Vimshopaka Shadvarga. Step 6 the count of
   links to the subject and dasha activity at the as-of date.
2. The highest value wins that step and gets one vote. A tie shares the vote.
3. Steps 4 and 5 do not vote. Step 4 produces the owner adjusted ranking that is shown beside the mathematical
   one, and a check on whether the two agree. Steps 4 and 5 also produce flags such as combust, retrograde or
   lost planetary war on the winner.
4. Most votes wins. A tie in votes is broken by step 6, then step 1, then step 3, then step 2.
5. Rahu and Ketu vote only on step 6, since they have no Shadbala. A node winner is flagged as lower confidence.
6. The planet that wins enforces its result. The answer must state the winner, the vote detail, the adjusted
   ranking check and the flags.

Other proposals to confirm: (a) the owner adjustment is read as Cheshta bala set to 60 virupa for a retrograde
planet and to zero for a combust planet, and if both apply, combust is applied; (b) graha yuddha winner is by
lower longitude because Vednetra produces no latitude, so it is provisional; (c) subject links are counted one
point each, with no weights; (d) the subject presets in data/topics.json marked proposed are the engine
author's, not the owner's.

## 7. CALIBRATION RULES FROM OUTCOME FEEDBACK (fifth instruction round)

Added after calibration case 1 (see suj_engine/data/calibration_cases.json). The owner supplied the real
outcome of a native for whom the engine had answered job and strong higher education, and instructed that the
engine be updated with the learnings. These are the rules that came out of that comparison. Rules 7.1 and 7.2
extend the owner's section 2.0 worked example and are applied as the owner's. Rules 7.3 to 7.6 are the engine
author's reading of the same case and stay open to the owner's correction.

7.1 SUN OR FATHER'S WORK RULE. The owner's section 2.0 example says that planets in a house deliver through
their natural and functional significations, and gives the case of a native who does the karmas or work of the
father. Operationally: when the 9th lord and the 10th lord are together, or the Sun joins the 10th lord, or the
9th lord sits in the house of the 10th lord, the engine must test father's work or the family occupation as the
first candidate for livelihood. A combust 10th lord with the Sun is read as work under the father or the family
authority, and not as a job under an outside organisation, unless the family occupation is ruled out by other
evidence in the chart.

7.2 SIXTH HOUSE RULE. The 6th house is service and also debt, loans given and taken, interest and recovery of
dues. The 8th house adds other people's money, interest and usury. The 2nd and 11th are money held and gains.
For a livelihood question the engine must read the 6th, 8th, 2nd and 11th for the kind of work, and must not
treat a strong 6th house or a strong 6th lord as a job indicator by itself. Money lending, finance, loans,
recovery and credit trades show as a 6th lord, Saturn in the 8th or aspecting the 2nd, Mars in the 6th and a
strong Jupiter or Mercury.

7.3 JOB OR BUSINESS DECISION ORDER (author proposal). Step one, test the family occupation (7.1). Step two,
read the kind of work through the 6th, 8th, 2nd and 11th (7.2). Step three, only then compare service against
independent trade using the 6th and 7th houses and their lords. A hemmed or weak 7th house reduces independent
partnership business. It does not rule out an inherited or family business, which runs on the 9th, 10th and
the Sun, and not on the 7th.

7.4 TOPIC PRESETS. The planet strength tool must never judge a livelihood question on the 10th house alone. Use
the topics career or job_or_business in data/topics.json, which cover the houses 10, 6, 7, 2 and 11. In
calibration case 1 the 10th house only preset gave a win to a planet through the Shadbala tie break, and the
wider preset gave a win to a different planet on votes.

7.5 EDUCATION RULE. Capacity and continuity are separate findings. Capacity is read from D24, the 4th, 5th and
9th houses and the karakas Mercury and Jupiter. Continuity and inclination are read from the 5th lord and its
house, paap kartari on the 5th and 9th, nodes in the 4th, and the nature of the running and next dasha lords.
A 5th lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th, or kartari on the 5th and 9th, means a break, a change of stream or an early
stop is a live outcome. A running Ketu period means detachment from formal study. The engine may promise a
degree level on capacity but must not promise a further postgraduate step unless continuity also supports it.

7.6 DISRUPTION WORDING. When the damage found is a kartari or a malefic hold on the education houses, the
engine states a break or change of stream as a possible outcome, and not only delay.

7.7 LIMITS. Calibration case 1 is one native and the outcome was known when the weights were changed, so it
shows an error in weighting and does not prove the new weighting. More cases must be logged in
calibration_cases.json and tested before these weights are treated as settled.
