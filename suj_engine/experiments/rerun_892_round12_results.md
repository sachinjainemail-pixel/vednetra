## Why this is not a gain

1. The pessimism is structural. No hypothesis reached 50, including marriage, where the 7th lord is in a kendra in exchange with the Lagna lord and
   eleven strict windows exist. The hits on work and on the father come from the same lean. They are not evidence of skill.
2. View 2 was made after seeing the first output. The first procedure added a weakness for every involved planet, including malefic aspectors. A weak
   malefic does less harm, which its smaller weight already shows, so the fix is principled, and it changed the scores by 0 to 8 points. No hypothesis
   crossed 50 in either view. Both are printed.
3. Suspected causes, none tested. The real strength S centres near 45 to 50, so most planets sit on the weak side of the owner's rule that a weak carrier turns
   against. The factors 1.0 for a trine lord and 0.5 for a kendra lord against 1.0 for a trik lord lean against. The line at 50 assumes the evidence
   is generated evenly for and against, and it is not.
4. Within this chart the scores rank as Education 48, Marriage 47, Father 45, Siblings 37, Mother 35, Work 33. The true yes answers are Education,
   Marriage, Siblings and Mother, the true no answers are Work and the Father. A line near 40 would separate four of six, which is the kind of
   statement that must be checked on other charts, not set from this one.
5. Dasha lords now, Laghu Parashari. Mahadasha Jupiter sadhaka, Antardasha Mercury neutral, Pratyantardasha Rahu neutral but a causer of expenditure, Sookshma Mercury
