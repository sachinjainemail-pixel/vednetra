# QUAD DASHA REPORT — the contract
*What the export guarantees, what it refuses, and what every number in it is worth.
Read this before handing a report to the Quad Dasha project.*

## 2. THE SEVEN CORRECTIONS — asserted in §0 of every report

Each settled against primary text. **If a user, a book, or another piece of software contradicts
one of these, the engine is right** — say so and cite.

1. **Kaal Chakra balance is against the WHOLE package span** (100/85/83/86), never against its
   first dasha. *BPHS 46/92–93; three worked examples in two translations.*
2. **Kaal Chakra has NINE antardasas per dasha, not twelve** — the nine signs of the native's own
   package, in package order, cycled to open on the dasha sign, each
   `dasha_yrs × antar_yrs ÷ span`. *BPHS Ch.64; 27/27 printed cells.* This is **not** Shakti Mohan
   Singh's variant; his R043–R051 and R054 are falsified by those tables.
3. **The Kaal Chakra package REPEATS when exhausted.** It does not advance to the next pada's
   package — deha and jeeva are natal invariants, and advancing would change them mid-life.
4. **Chara length-counting goes in groups of THREE:** Ar/Ta/Ge forward, Cn/Le/Vi reverse,
   Li/Sc/Sg forward, Cp/Aq/Pi reverse. *Rao's own worked table; matches BPHS's odd/even pada
   rule.* Reading it as two groups of six, or as odd/even signs, produces errors of exactly
   `12 − correct`.
5. **Chara: no ±1 year for exaltation or debilitation, no fractional years.** Max 12, min 1,
   never 0.
6. **Seven Chara karakas, never eight.** Ties resolve at seconds.
7. **Lahiri ayanamsa only.** Another ayanamsa reorders the karakas and can turn an Atmakaraka
   into a Darakaraka.

## 4. THE NOISE FLOOR — why §10 exists

Measured across 20 charts by perturbing the birth time one minute:

> **2.92 months of drift in every Kaal Chakra dasha boundary per minute of birth-time error**
> (range 2.41–3.60 across charts). This independently confirms Shakti Mohan Singh's own
> "2 months to 4 months 18 days" figure, from a different direction.

| birth-time error | KCD boundary drift |
|---|---|
| ±1 min | ±0.24 y |
| ±5 min | **±1.22 y** |
| ±15 min | ±3.65 y |
| ±60 min | **±14.6 y** |

That last row is why a timezone error is fatal and not cosmetic: one hour moves a Kaal Chakra
dasha by nearly fifteen years.

percent of the time **will** survive 500 charts with no visible failure. *"It never fails"* and
*"it has no predictive content"* are the same observation seen from two sides.

**And the narrow rules outperform the permissive ones.** Strict AD-lordship (null 11.9%) measured
1.15 where the loose form (null 59.7%) measured 0.99. **Looseness is not a safety margin — it is
what destroys the information. A rule with a 95% base rate can be deleted with no loss.**

## 6. WHAT FAILED NOTABLY — do not present these as findings

- **Laghu Parashari's central career rule** (yogakaraka/trikona MD with a *related* auspicious AD
  → career elevation): **lift 0.47**, the worst result in the study.
- **Rao's Sagittarius/Leo rise-and-fall rashis** → amplitude events: **0.70**.
- **Rao's Atmakaraka-dasha significance**: **0.82**.
- **Maraka rules in all three systems that have one** — Vimshottari 0.82, Yogini 0.82, KCD
  deha/jeeva 0.82. **Death does not select for maraka periods here.**
- **Kaal Chakra's three gaits** — the system's signature, and the basis of Rao's most famous
  prediction: **0.81–0.95** across every specification tried, including the disruption-only
  variant that is the fairest reading of SMS's own claim.
- **Goel's complete transit funnel**: 0.97, on an 86.6% base rate.

The only rule that pointed the right way was the **sector relationship**: lift 1.08 (p = 0.11),
rising to 1.16 on the 13 best-dated charts. It needs ~106 events to resolve and there were 69.

## 8. REFUSALS AND NON-REFUSALS

**The report never dates a death**, and neither should anything built on it. Longevity and maraka
analysis may be discussed as the classical texts frame them — including that *no maraka period
kills before the term of longevity is used up* — but no date. Note that the book which supplies
the four-level Gulika death-date chain tests it on a real death and **marks itself 3 of 5 rules
correct**: its longevity class said "middle life" and the native died at 89.

**Grave questions are not refusals.** A medical, psychiatric or mortality question selects which
classical template to run and how carefully to phrase it — never a refusal to engage.

**Never claim** a validated hit rate, a percentage accuracy, or that any of these systems has been
proven. No source in this corpus computed a base rate, so no source's success claim is evidence.

## 9. LIMITS

1. **Ephemeris.** Positions come from the bundled VedNetra. Against the project's Python engine
   the two casters differ by **1.4–3.5 arcminutes on the Moon** on the validation charts — 2.6–6.3
   minutes of equivalent birth time, up to **~18 months of Kaal Chakra drift**. On Indira Gandhi's
   death date that was enough to change which KCD dasha was running, while Vimshottari, Chara and
   Yogini agreed exactly across both. §10's stability row is the governing fact.
2. **Rahu is the mean node**, ±~2° against the true node. It matters near a sign boundary — and
   there it changes the Chara Aquarius/Scorpio length and the maraka board.
3. **Dignity, Shadbala, combustion and blemish are not computed**, and neither are most divisional
   charts. SMS, Rao and Goel all condition their rules on these, so the frames in §2–§5 are the
   **skeletons** of their rules, not the rules.
4. **No transits, deliberately.** 34 transit rules were tested; the best was indistinguishable
   from the best of 400 randomly invented ones (1.54 against a noise 99th percentile of 1.42).
5. **Whole-sign houses throughout.** No bhava cusps anywhere.
6. **The benchmark population** was 18 twentieth-century political and royal figures with
   published birth times. It cannot support a sweeping negative, and it is not a general
   population. The honest scope of the null is: *these specific rules, in these forms, on that
   population, carry no detectable information about event timing.*

## 10. WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT

Pre-registered **narrow** rules (base rates in the 10–40% band, where discriminative capacity is
high), on **300–500 events** from charts with **birth times known to the minute**, scored **out of
sample** — predictions made before the dates are looked up. At ±1 min the KCD drift is ±0.24 y,
which is *inside* a 1.1-year bhukti, so minute-accurate data would also make the bhukti testable
for the first time. The harness exists; it needs the data, not more code.
