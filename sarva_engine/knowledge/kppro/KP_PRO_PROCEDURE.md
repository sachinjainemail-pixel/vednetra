# KP Pro procedure for the SARVA engine

Working summary of KP-PRO-METHODOLOGY.docx, the owner's detailed booklet on SHRI GANESHA BLESSED KP PRO, instruction version v15 of
21 October 2026, booklet dated 16 November 2026, 33 sections. The booklet is the authority and this is the English map. The text is
in KP-PRO-METHODOLOGY.txt and the section index with line numbers in KP-PRO-METHODOLOGY.index.tsv. This booklet extends the existing
KP Pro column, it does not add a column. The rule files themselves are in the sibling repo, KP-Pro-sutton/knowledge/kp_pro, with the
v15 instructions in KP-PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS-v15.md. KP 15 is not stored as a separate file there. The booklet's own rule 4.2 says
rebuild a missing KP 12, 14 or 15 from sections 7, 8 and 9 of the instructions, and the v15 instructions do hold the Rule of 10.

## Authority order (section 3)

KP 14 first, on stability, fructification, prior, calibration and the five missed rule families. Then KP 11 version 4 on the promise
gate, KP 12 on strength and naming, KP 13 on rashi and graha nature, KP 15 on Rule of 10 mechanics only, then the instruction text,
then the numbered files, last old chat precedents. Name every conflict in one line. The VedNetra B4 verdict column is never carried
into the report because it is a script product.

## Preflight, eight runs and the eight box gate (section 4, always)

1 VedNetra KP System Report at the stated birth time. 2 and 3 the same at plus and minus 5 minutes for the stability check. 4 BALA
table of the six limbs. 5 grain of the birth time and admissibility of every main cusp. 6 plausibility prior, computed and written
before reading the chart. 7 rule family search in the project knowledge including rashi, graha nature and the five missed families.
8 Rule of 10 layers, exactly ten per option, frozen before the chart is looked at. No prediction is published until all eight boxes
are filled. If a run truly cannot run, say so.

## Thirteen fixed settings (section 5)

KP ayanamsa only, which is Lahiri minus 6 arcminutes. Placidus to the degree. Every point read sign then star then sub. The stability
tier is fixed, a TIME ROBUST question is not decided by a cusp sub lord unless that CSL survives the declared grain. Sutton, Rashi
and Graha nature layers are mandatory in every reading. ONE LAYER runs only when no point exists. No retrograde veto, a retrograde
planet only gives delay or repetition. The colour tool is mandatory, sign, nakshatra and pada of both promise planets. BALA never
creates or erases a promise and never separates house groups. Existence is decided only by the KP 11 gate, whether it has happened
only by the dasha window. The prior is computed and written first. Every answer carries HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW and CHART DECIDED or
PRIOR DOMINANT. Rule of 10 is mandatory on every option. Mean nodes.

## Prior and the four bands (section 8)

Marriage prior by age and sex, for example a woman under 22 0.10 and a man under 22 0.03, 35 and over 0.95. Parent alive prior
0.95 under 30 down to 0.30 at 60 and over. Defaults, siblings mode 3, education mode graduate, height middle band, residence birth
state. Chart and prior are merged by the significator NET band. STRONG, NET 6 or more, the chart decides with high trust. ABSENT,
minus 2 or less, the chart decides with high trust. PRESENT, 2 to 5, the chart decides with reduced trust. UNDECIDED, minus 1 to 1,
the prior decides and the answer is labelled PRIOR DOMINANT.

## Stability tier (section 9)

Stable, planet sign, star, sub and house, the lists of houses a planet signifies, cusp sign and sign lord, the four step significator
list, Vimshottari. Unstable, the sub lords of the twelve cusps and everything built on them. A 5 minute shift changed 240 of 240 cusp
sub lords. TIME CRITICAL questions, horary or a window under one month, are decided by the CSL. TIME ROBUST questions, married or not,
education, parents, are decided by the stable items. Admissibility, a cusp is inadmissible if its sub changes within the declared grain,
and then the gate is advisory only.

## Promise gate, KP 11 version 4 (section 10)

Layer A is the cusp sub lord and the houses it signifies, weight 2. Layer B is the star lord of that CSL and its houses, weight 1. The
CSL's own sub lord is not counted in the promise decision. GATE NET is twice A's supporting houses minus A's denying houses plus B's
supporting minus B's denying. 6 or more PROMISED AND CONFIRMED, 2 to 5 PROMISED, minus 1 to 1 GATE UNDECIDED and go to the significator
count and the prior, minus 2 or less DENIED. No OBSTRUCTED band, no retrograde veto. A node is represented by conjunction, then aspect,
then star lord, then sign lord, the star lord before the sign lord.

## Significators and NET (section 11)

Four steps per house, A planets in the star of the house occupants, strongest, B the occupants, C planets in the star of the house
lord, D the house lord, E conjunct or aspecting planets as support. Rahu and Ketu are strongest when in the sign of a lord or in the
star of a significator. Each significator is filtered by its own sub. Ties are broken by BALA, never intuition. NET is the rank
weighted sum over the top three significators of supporting houses minus denying houses. 6 or more STRONG, 2 to 5 PRESENT, minus 1
to 1 UNDECIDED, minus 2 or less ABSENT. A house signified by 7 or more planets is a noise house and drops one level.

## Rule of 10 (section 17)

Every option gets exactly ten plain word prerequisite layers written before any planet is read and never changed after. Four refusal
tests, outcome, anchor, direction, reading. Layer score is natal 0.7 times weighted house condition plus 0.3 times karaka strength,
plus half the dasha part at weights 0.25, 0.35 and 0.4 for dasha, bhukti and pratyantar, plus a quarter of the transit part,
Jupiter and Saturn on the layer's houses. SATISFIED at the chart median, 1.1 for a SUPPORT layer, 0.6 for an AFFLICT layer. Rank first
by the number of SATISFIED layers, then strong planets up to 0.3 and rajayoga up to 0.2 per layer, a lead under 2.0 is NARROW. Three
tables in every analysis, block 4A.

Arbitration against the KP spine (17.8). 1 an option excluded by a DENIED gate or failed fructification stays excluded, R10 prints it
and cannot revive it. 2 a firm spine, one option clearly ahead on gate band and NET, R10 does not change the answer. 3 a thin spine,
two or more options in the same gate band with NET within 2, or the gate UNDECIDED, R10 depth breaks the tie. 4 if still equal, BALA
breaks it, never intuition. 5 an invalid or mostly UNTESTABLE layer set is NOT SEPARATED and sits out. R10 has not been measured on
the 122 question ledger and is PROVISIONAL.

## Fructification and transit (sections 19 and 20)

A promise is possible, an event is that it has happened. Walk the mahadasha order and the bhuktis inside each qualifying one. A
qualifying window is a dasha bhukti pair where both lords score positive for the house group and the bhukti is the subject's. If the
first qualifying window has begun, the event has happened. If no qualifying window exists at any age the event does not fructify in
this life and that overturns the prior. Age bands from KP 14, school 5 to 18, first degree 17 to 23, postgraduate 21 to 27, marriage
from 17 for a woman and 18 for a man, first child from 19 and 20, livelihood from 20, leaving the birthplace from 18. Transit, the Sun
sets the month, the Moon the day, Jupiter and Saturn the year band, the rising sign the hour, passing through the star and the sub
of the significators and not the sign alone. Sade Sati is read separately with the KSK and Sutton disagreement stated.

## Output and the 17 mandatory rules (sections 26 and 28)

Seven output blocks and nothing else, preflight line, BALA table, planet significators, cusp significators, the three R10 tables, the
24 layer lines, the final answer in Hindi in three time groups, trace file. Mandatory rules include never pleasing the user, no step
skipped, dasha and transit before any final answer, always a concrete answer, three SWOT events for a bare date, no forbidden
question, the CSL's sub lord never in the promise decision, no claim of accuracy beyond the ledger, R10 never skipped thinned or
reversed and never presented as proof, and a line saying whether the chart, the prior or R10 gave the answer.

## Measured record (section 27, the owner's ledger)

122 questions, 20 charts. Random 24.8 percent. Prior alone 39.3. Version 13 27.0. Version 14 corrected 47.5 percent, 54.2 in the first
half and 41.3 in the second, honest expectation on unseen charts 41 to 45 percent, against the group's average member at 38 and the
quiz winner near 71. On marital status, siblings, residence and parents, 55 of the 122 questions, the chart added nothing over the
prior. Coborns and height are declared failures, answered from the prior with LOW trust. BALA and the numeric gate were not measured
separately and R10 is unmeasured.

## How SARVA reconciles the booklet with the SARVA contract

1. The prior. The KP Pro method itself starts from a computed prior and lets the chart decide when the NET band is STRONG, PRESENT or
   ABSENT. That is KP Pro's own rule, so the KP Pro cell may legitimately be PRIOR DOMINANT when the chart is UNDECIDED, and the
   EVIDENCE line must say so. The ban in the contract is on the kpredict ask default output, which never lets the chart in at all, and
   on using priors for the other six columns.
2. The cell is the answer for the stated birth time, the first of the three time groups. If the plus and minus 5 minute answers differ,
   the EVIDENCE line names the difference and the cell does not change.
3. The R10 comparison with Chawdhri and Sutton in block 4A is NOT RUN under SARVA because no methodology reads another's verdict.
   Sutton inside KP Pro, the KP 7 to 10 layer, is part of KP Pro and does run.
4. Chart source. The booklet names the VedNetra KP System Report. The sibling repo found that VedNetra falls back to Porphyry for the
   intermediate cusps in most cases, so the cusp sub lords of houses 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11 and 12 are checked against true Placidus
   from kpredict, and any difference is named in one line as a declared conflict, as booklet section 3 requires.
5. The Hindi devotional style, the Hindi final answer and the three group layout are not needed in SARVA. The EVIDENCE line carries
   the confidence word, HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW, and the basis, CHART DECIDED, PRIOR DOMINANT or R10 DECIDED, and the guard checks both.
6. Natal only. Horary questions need a judgment moment and are outside SARVA, which reads one native for all seven columns.
