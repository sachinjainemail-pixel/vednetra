# KCIL procedure for the SARVA engine

Working summary of KCIL-METHODOLOGY-V2.6.pdf, the owner's 65 page Hindi complete summary of the KCIL project, version 2.6, dated 16
November 2026. KCIL is S.P. Khullar's Sub Sub and Cuspal Interlinks method. The PDF is the authority, this is the English map. The
text is in KCIL-METHODOLOGY-V2.6.txt and the part index with line numbers in KCIL-METHODOLOGY-V2.6.index.tsv. This extends the
existing KCIL column and adds no column. The rule files and the full v2.6 instructions are in the sibling repo, KP-Pro-sutton,
knowledge/kcil, KCIL-CUSTOM-INSTRUCTIONS-v2.6.md and KCIL-1 to KCIL-10. Where the PDF cites sections 16, 17 and 18, those sections
are in the v2.6 instructions file, not in the PDF. The PDF holds parts 1 to 10 and ends inside part 10. It refers to a part 11
that is not in it.

## Five source books (part 1)

KCIL TA, True Astrology, the dictionary of meanings and never a source of verdicts. KCIL KAL, Kalamsa and Cuspal Interlinks Theory,
the main reference. KCIL KTL, Key to Learn Sub Sub and Cuspal Interlinks Theory, 15 lessons. KCIL HOR, Horary Astrology and Cuspal
Interlinks. KCIL BTR, Your True Horoscope Birth Time Rectification, whose 2009 amendments override the earlier books. Priority, BTR
chapter 5 first, then KAL, KTL, HOR, TA. Where TA and the CIL books conflict on method, the CIL books win.

## Seven fixed settings and the chart validity check (part 3, step zero)

1 Khullar ayanamsa by formula, zero year 292 not 291, computed hourly. 2 Placidus with cusps as house starts. 3 geocentric latitude, not
geodetic. 4 true nodes. 5 nutation applied. 6 depth to Prana, the fifth level, SSSS. 7 longitude to the second. Uranus, Neptune and
Pluto are excluded from every test. A chart not cast on these is not a KCIL chart, say so and ask for a recast, never go on silently.
The only exception is a horary number from 1 to 2193, which needs no birth data. Coordinates and time zone are chart material and not
evidence. Khullar's smallest unit, the sub sub arc, is about 2 arcminutes, which the Ascendant crosses in about 8 seconds.

Measured by the owner on 20 real charts. At the recorded minute, an average of 10.8 of 12 cusps change their sub sub lord within 30
seconds. Only 1 chart in 20 passed all six rectification tests at the recorded time and the sex test failed in 12. A time within 10
minutes passing all six exists in every chart, but an average of 28 distinct 2 second bands pass all six and 64 percent of answers flip
among them. A birth time to the minute is a band and not a decision.

## Closed system (part 3)

Not used in KCIL. Retrograde, combustion, aspects in the Western or Indian sense, exaltation and debilitation, strength and Shadbala,
planet age, yogas and rajayogas, divisional charts and navamsa, Ashtakavarga, Western progressions, returns and harmonics. Also
forbidden, outside facts such as almanac, festival, news or what a family usually does that day. KCIL is never mixed with KP, they
use the same words and differ on what decides. KP's rule that a sub lord redirects the dasha lord is named and rejected.

## Promise and the three gates (part 5)

The cuspal sub lord decides the promise and the sub sub lord is the verdict. Three levels read for a cusp's SSL, its star lord and
its contacts give the promise, its sub lord gives the condition, its own sub sub lord gives the final result. Three gates every
time. Gate 1 the Ascendant, the SSL of the Ascendant must link to the main cusp. Gate 2 the main cusp, its SSL linked to the houses of
the event group. Gate 3 the 11th cusp, fulfilment, its SSL linked to the main cusp. Lagna and main cusp must both pass. The Ascendant's
promise ranks above every cusp. A failed gate is an answer and not an obstacle, and a thing not in the promise is not timed.
Direct and indirect linkage by the 2009 definition, direct, the star lord of the SSL sits in the main cusp and its sub lord shows in
a supporting cusp. Indirect, the star lord sits in a supporting cusp and its sub lord shows in the main cusp.

## Timing (part 7)

Promise, dasha, transit and progression must all agree. Five levels, Dasa, Bhukti, Antara, Sookshma, Prana, the dasha lords of a chart
are the Moon's co-rulers. The birth Prana lord is the Moon's SSSS lord. A descending ladder of permission, the Dasa permits, the Bhukti
chooses, the Antara confirms, Sookshma and Prana start the event, a lower level cannot give what a higher level refused. A dasha lord
may give a major event in its own bhukti if it signifies both the main and supporting cusps. The frequency of an event decides which
level is decisive.

## Event matrix and playbooks (part 10) and horary (part 9)

Each event has a main cusp and supporting cusps, with bracketed houses counted in the promise check as well as timing, some events have
two main cusps, and any profession has the field cusp and the 10th. A five gate algorithm, truth, promise, fulfilment, timing, agent.
Playbooks for ascendant, wealth, education, disease, marriage, profession, children, from KAL chapters 6 to 12. Horary and its number
method, part 9, need a judgment moment and are outside SARVA's natal scope.

## Rectification (part 8)

Six tests, the Ascendant's co-rulers linking 1, 7 or 10, the Ascendant SSL related to the Moon's star lord, the sex test, the Moon's
co-rulers linking 1, 7 or 10, the Moon's levels signifying 1, 7 or 10, and the birth Prana lord signifying two of 10, 1 and 7. Per the
v2.6 instructions section 17.1, run it before any verdict, it is an operation and not a disclaimer. If the six pass at the given
time, stop. Otherwise cast at 0, minus and plus 30 s, 60 s, 2 m, 4 m, 6 m and 10 m, at most 13 casts, the sex test is a hard gate.

## Measured record (part 1 and the v2.6 instructions)

Version 2.4 on 20 blind charts, 123 questions, 34 right, 27.6 percent, chance 24.8, always option 1 also 27.6, best fixed answer per
family 42.3. Answers with no flipping material SSL, determinate, scored 44.4 percent against 25.1 random. Non determinate answers

is not measured and is provisional, it ranks only if its regression test shows a gain of about 8 points or more.

## How SARVA reconciles KCIL with the SARVA contract

1. Determinacy. KCIL answers a non determinate question with two candidates and forbids merging them. SARVA needs one cell. The cell
   is the candidate the KCIL closing rule of section 16.30 prefers, the lower grade, the smaller count, the event not yet occurred, the
   person still alive. The EVIDENCE line says TWO CANDIDATES, names the SSL that flips and the seconds, and names the other candidate.
   A determinate answer says STABLE.
2. The no rule register of section 17.8. For height, graded build, religion, a named city, a child's sex and a relative's life span
   the corpus has no rule. KCIL says declare it. SARVA still needs one cell, so the cell is the option the nearest real rule
   supports, and the EVIDENCE line says NO RULE REGISTER and names that rule.
3. The Rule of 10 of KCIL section 18 leads the ranking by KCIL's own order when its layers were written before the chart, and its
   margin is CLEAR, NARROW or TIE. A tie goes to the rule in item 1.
4. The honest prior line of section 17.9 is stated once in the SARVA LIMITS line, not in each cell.
5. KCIL reads its own chart cast on Khullar settings and never reads another methodology's verdict. Neither the KP report nor a VedNetra
   Lahiri chart is a KCIL chart.
6. Horary questions are out of scope. Birth time rectification is in scope and its outcome goes in the EVIDENCE line.
