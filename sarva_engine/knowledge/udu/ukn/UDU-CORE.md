## Ashvini — core (15)

- **ASH-0175** · nak Ashvini · ASH p78 · +/stated
  **IF** Ashvini falls in the caste of Brahma and the Marichi gotra
  **THEN** Native is loaded with innovative and creative ideas and is taught religious practice, ritual, etiquette, prayer and yajna in all domains of life.
