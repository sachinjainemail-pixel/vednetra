  **B02-R011 — Reject the double agent.** Reject any planet that signifies **both the positive and the negative houses** of the matter — e.g. for marriage, one signifying **2, 7, 11** *and also* **1, 6, 10** (the 12ths to 2, 7, 11). *If such a planet's dasa is running, in some cases marriage simply does not occur, and only the next period gives it.* *(p.34)*
- **B02-R028** · B02 · keyword match
  **B02-R028 — Exemption for supportive houses.** Every question has one **main** house and some **supportive** houses. If the sub lord signifies a **supportive** house, it counts as **favourable even if it falls 4/5/7/8/9/12 away** from the main house. *(e.g. marriage: 7 is main, 2 and 11 supportive — a sub lord signifying 2 or 11 stays favourable.)* *(p.104)*
