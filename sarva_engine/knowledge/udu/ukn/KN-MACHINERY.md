# KN corpus, universal machinery

**610 rules.** These carry no life event of their own. They are the clock itself, dasha selection, synchronization, balance arithmetic, break tests, delivery rules, transit machinery and the precision apparatus. They apply to all twelve protocols.

## B01 (22)

- **B01-R001**
  **B01-R001 — The dasa begins from the stronger of Lagna and the 7th house.** These two are the *satya-peethas* (seats of truth). Everything downstream depends on getting this right. *(p.1)*
- **B01-R002**
  **B01-R002 — Test 1 (occupancy).** The sign holding a planet — or holding more planets — is stronger than the sign with none or fewer. *(p.1)*
- **B01-R003**
  **B01-R003 — Test 2 (aspect quality).** The sign aspected by Mercury, Jupiter, or by its own lord is stronger. *(p.1)*
- **B01-R004**
  **B01-R004 — Test 3 (dignity of occupants).** If occupancy and aspect are equal, compare the *status* of the occupying planets — exaltation, Mulatrikona, own sign, etc. The sign with better-dignified occupants wins. *(p.1)*
- **B01-R005**
  **B01-R005 — Test 4 (natural strength of modality).** If still equal, or if both signs are empty: **dual > fixed > movable**. *Caveat stated by the author: this test does not work when quadrants are being compared.* *(p.1)*
- **B01-R007**
  **B01-R007 — Test 6 (longer dasa period wins).** If still equal, the sign yielding the **higher dasa period** is stronger. Count from the sign to its lord **zodiacally if the sign is odd-footed**, **anti-zodiacally if even-footed**. Odd-footed: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius. Even-footed: the remaining six. For Scorpio and Aquarius use the stronger of the two lords (see §B). *(p.1–2)*
- **B01-R008**
  **B01-R008 — Test 7 (Atmakaraka lordship).** If still equal, the sign whose lord is the **Atmakaraka** is stronger. *(p.2)*
- **B01-R010**
  **B01-R010 — Both lords in the same sign: no conflict.** The count runs from the sign to that shared sign, whichever lord you name. *(p.2)*
- **B01-R011**
  **B01-R011 — One lord in the sign itself, the other elsewhere: use the one placed elsewhere.** The dasa is estimated from the lord placed in the *other* sign. *(p.2)*
- **B01-R012**
  **B01-R012 — Both lords elsewhere: the stronger occupied sign decides.** Compare the two signs the lords occupy (using §A's strength tests); the lord sitting in the stronger sign gives the dasa period. *(p.2)*
- **B01-R013**
  **B01-R013 — Occupied signs equal in strength: take the longer period.** The lord contributing the **larger dasa period** is used. *(p.2)*
- **B01-R014**
  **B01-R014 — Divide every rasi dasa into three parts.** The three parts express, respectively: (a) the sign/house itself, (b) its lord, (c) the planets occupying and aspecting that sign. *(p.85)*

In the two thirds not claimed by the sign itself, the benefic influence takes the earlier slot. So among lord / occupant / aspecting planet, whichever is **most benefic** delivers in the earlier remaining third, and the malefic one in the later. *(p.85)*

  **B02-R006 — Method 2: the significator's own sub-lord decides.** If a significator's **sub lord is harmonious** to the matters shown by its star lord and by itself → it gives favourable results.   **B02-R007 — Method 3: sub-lord must itself be in the significator list.** Check each significator: **is its sub lord included among the significators?** If yes → keep it as fruitful; if no → drop it. *(p.35)*

RP order of strength: 1. Ascendant **star** lord 2. Ascendant **sign** lord 3. Moon **star** lord 4. Moon **sign** lord 5. **Day** lord (sunrise to sunrise) Taken at the **moment of judgment**, and used **only when there is a genuine need to decide between significators**. *(p.34)*

  **B02-R012 — The 12th-house rejection rule (general).** Reject any significator signifying the **12th to the house under consideration**. A planet whose **sub lord** signifies that negating house is likewise unfruitful. Planets in **6, 8, 12 to the concerned house** do not contribute; **any house 6, 8 or 12 to another house negates it.** *(p.35)*

Conversely a planet **not among the sub lords** of the concerned houses is the **weakest**. *(p.35)*
- **B02-R014**

  **B02-R015 — The Dasa is the loose filter; the Bhukthi is where care is needed.** **Any** planet signifying the matter can be taken as **Dasanatha regardless of its strength**. But the **Bhukthi must be a planet connected to the Dasanatha — and no other.** Likewise the **Anthranatha must be favourably connected to the Bhukthinatha *and* to the Dasa**, and should be of **higher strength as a significator**. *(p.100)*

A good aspect formed during retrogression is **debilitated**; an adverse aspect formed during retrogression is **accentuated**. Retrogression brings out introspective rather than objective qualities, and is generally **synonymous with belated or denied benefits**. *(p.31–32)*

  **B02-R055 — Stationary = very strong.** A planet's effect is **usually very strong when stationary** upon the place of a radical or progressed planet, or in aspect to it. *(p.32)*

## B05 (40)

- **B05-R002**
  **B05-R002 — The best case.** A powerful planet **not inimical to the Ascendant lord**, placed in a **benefic house from all three lagnas** (birth, Moon, Sun), and **in his own sign**, gives **very good results in his Dasha and Antardasha**. *(p.43)*

  **B05-R004 — Natural benefic/malefic classification does not govern Vimshottari.** Natural benefics are Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Moon; natural malefics Mars, Sun, Saturn, plus Rahu and Ketu. **But this categorisation does not work in the Vimshottari system, because a bad planet cannot remain bad for ever, and the same is true of good planets.** Use the functional grouping below instead. *(p.44)*

  **B05-R005 — Auspicious = lords of the trines.** **Even malefics, when they become trinal lords, give good results in their Dasha and Antardasha.** *(p.44)*
- **B05-R006**
  **B05-R006 — Inauspicious = lords of the 3rd, 6th and 11th.** These **give bad results throughout their Dasha period**. **Even Jupiter and Venus, if they own these houses, are capable of giving bad results.** *(p.44)*
- **B05-R007**
  **B05-R007 — Neutral = lords of the kendras.** A **benefic owning a kendra loses its benefic potentiality and becomes neutral**; a **malefic likewise loses its natural qualities and becomes neutral.** *(p.44)*
- **B05-R008**
  **B05-R008 — Dual nature = lords of the 2nd and 12th.** They give good or bad results depending on position; specifically, **the 2nd and 12th lords can give the results of a Rajayoga in the Dasha or Antardasha of yogakaraka planets.** *(p.45)*

  **B05-R009 — The good and bad house-lord sets for dhana.** Lords of **1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11** form a very good **Dhana yoga** when powerful. Lords of **3, 6, 8, 12** are inauspicious and **give losses when they are powerful**. *(p.46–47)*

  **B05-R010 — What lets an auspicious lord actually deliver.** Lords of the auspicious houses give auspicious results when: in **own, exaltation or friendly sign**; **in a kendra from the Ascendant**; **in an auspicious varga in Navamsha**; **retrograde**; **associated with auspicious planets**; **with sufficient Shadbala**. *(p.47)*
- **B05-R011**
  **B05-R011 — What makes an inauspicious lord bite.** A lord of the inauspicious houses gives the bad results of his lordship if **debilitated, in an inimical sign, associated with another malefic, or weak in Shadbala.** *(p.47)*

  **B05-R012 — The separative planets.** **Sun, Saturn, Rahu and Mars** give **separation from the events of the houses they influence** — unless their malefic effect is cancelled or balanced by benefic influence. *(p.45)*
- **B05-R013**
  **B05-R013 — The Sun is the mildest of these.** Sun causes separation **only when** he is influenced by another malefic by aspect or association, **or through the malefic nature of his dispositor, or through a malefic placed in Leo**. *(p.45, p.47)*
- **B05-R015**
  **B05-R015 — *Kendriya* effects — the 10th-from-a-planet rule.** Just as the Ascendant is always under the influence of the 10th lord and of planets in the 10th, **every planet remains under the influence of whatever sits in the 10th from itself**. Consequently, **the good or bad results during a planet's sub-period depend on the nature of the planets placed in the 10th from it.** *(p.45)* Corollary: **the evil results of a planet are also seen in the sub-period of any planet from which it is placed in the 10th.** *(p.47)*
- **B05-R016**
  **B05-R016 — The nodes and their dispositors.** Rahu and Ketu have **5th, 7th and 9th aspects**, and unless associated with other planets they give **the results of Saturn and Mars respectively**. They give **the results of their dispositor**, and carry the effects of planets they are **conjoined with or aspected by** — and these effects appear in their **aspect value** too. Reciprocally, **the dispositor of Rahu/Ketu is itself influenced by them and carries their good and bad effects into its own aspects — results seen in the main/sub-periods of such dispositors.** *(p.45–46)*
- **B05-R017**
  **B05-R017 — A house lord inherits from its occupants.** The lord of a house **remains under the influence of the planets placed in that house**; if those are Saturn, Mars etc., the lord **may cause separation from the events of that house, or of the houses it aspects.** *(p.46)*

  **B05-R018 — Elemental nature and its variability.** Houses 1/5/9 and their lords are **fiery**; 2/6/10 **earthy**; 3/7/11 **airy**; 4/8/12 **watery**.   **B06-R034 — Even an exalted planet stays weak in the 6th, 8th or 12th.** *(p.52)*

  **B06-R035 — The four (plus two) kinds of combination.** Parashara: (1) **exchange** of houses; (2) **mutual aspect**; (3) a planet **aspected by its dispositor**; (4) **conjunction** (same sign). Mantreshwara adds: (5) two planets **in an angle from each other**; (6) two planets **in trine from each other**. *(p.52–53)*

  **B06-R036 — Rajayogas, in descending order of power.** (1) **kendra lord + trikona lord**; (2) **trikona lord + trikona lord**; (3) **kendra lord + kendra lord**. Additionally, **Rahu or Ketu combining with a kendra or trikona lord *in* a kendra or trikona forms a powerful Rajayoga.** *(p.53)*
- **B06-R037**
  **B06-R037 — Dhana yogas.** (1) **2nd + 11th lord**; (2) **2nd lord + kendra/trikona lord**; (3) **11th lord + kendra/trikona lord**. **Rahu/Ketu with the 2nd or 11th lord in a kendra, trikona or the 11th also gives a dhana yoga.** *(p.53)*

  **B06-R042 — Read a transit as you would a natal placement.** Planets transiting the **6th, 8th or 12th become weak**. *(p.76)*

- **B06-R045**

## B07 (16)

- **B07-R001**
  **B07-R001 — SCD is a supportive, not an independent, dasha.** K.N. Rao's own framing in the preface: it *"can give broad hints of coming events in the life of an individual, country or a Prashna… **It may not be fully dependable as an independent dasha in some cases.** Though, if we use it along with Vimshottari Dasha or Char Dasha, it can give good results in many cases."* *(Preface)*
- **B07-R003**
  **B07-R003 — The intended workflow.** Time the event with **Vimshottari first**, then **validate with SCD**. Rao's instruction: *"It should be used with Vimshottari dasha and transit."* *(p.60)*
- **B07-R005**
  **B07-R005 — Mahadasha: three signs, twelve years each, from the three lagnas.** 1. **First mahadasha** — the rasi in the **Lagna**. 2. **Second mahadasha** — the rasi where the **Moon** is placed. 3. **Third mahadasha** — the rasi where the **Sun** is placed. Each runs **12 years**; the three repeat **in the same order every 36 years**. *(p.60)*
- **B07-R006**
  **B07-R006 — Antardasha: twelve sub-periods of one year each.** The **first antardasha is of the same rasi as the mahadasha**; subsequent antardashas follow the signs **in zodiacal order** from it, one year each. *(p.60)*
- **B07-R007**
  **B07-R007 — Pratyantar dasha: twelve sub-sub-periods of one month each**, in the same sign order, beginning from the antardasha rasi. *(p.60)*
- **B07-R008**
  **B07-R008 — The dasha begins at birth, unadjusted.** There is no balance-at-birth computation — the first mahadasha starts on the birth date and runs a full 12 years. *(Demonstrated throughout the worked tables, p.64 ff.)*
- **B07-R009**
  **B07-R009 — The governing move: treat each dasha rasi as the Lagna.** For the mahadasha rasi, the antardasha rasi and the pratyantar rasi in turn, **re-count the houses from that sign** and judge the matter in question from there. *(Applied uniformly across all worked events, pp.68–73.)*
- **B07-R010**
  **B07-R010 — Read all three levels, and require agreement.** A prediction is made only when the **mahadasha sign, the antardasha sign and the pratyantar sign each independently show the event** from their own house-count. Where one level is silent the author says so explicitly. *(pp.68–73)*
- **B07-R015**
  **B07-R015 — An empty, unaspected dasha rasi whose lord is ill-placed is a negative indication.** *"Libra sign doesn't have any planet. It is also not aspected by any planet. Its Lord Venus is conjoined with Rahu and is ill-placed."* — used to explain a bereavement. *(p.70)*
- **B07-R022**
  **B07-R022 — Saints / people born with a mission** *(p.74)*: - Benefic influence on the **2nd house** - **Jupiter's influence on the 10th house / 10th lord from the Lagna** - **Jupiter's influence on the 10th house / 10th lord from the Moon** - **Jupiter's influence on the 10th house / 10th lord from the Lagnesh** - **Jupiter's influence on the 10th from the 10th (i.e. the 7th house)** - Connection of **PK, AK and AmK in D-1 and D-9** - **PAC connection of Lagna and Lagnesh with the 9th / 9th lord** (Jaimini aspect)

- **B07-R025**
  **B07-R025 — Royalty** *(p.156)*: a **strong 5th house from all three lagnas — Lagna, Moon Lagna and Sun Lagna** (good past-life karma); **many Rajayogas**; and **favourable dashas at the right time in life**.
- **B07-R026**
  **B07-R026 — Leaders (Presidents / Vice-Presidents)** *(p.177)*: - **Strong Sun, well-placed Moon, strong Lagna — all three lagnas strong with a strong Lagnesh** - **Benefic aspects of Jupiter and Saturn** (to connect instantly with people) - **Strong Mars** (energy to lead) - Strong **9th, 7th (pada-prapti) and 10th houses** - Good yogas, **dhana yogas and good-quality Rajayogas** - **Timing:** *"dashas of the Lagna, 5th, 9th and 10th house lords do wonders"* — at the right age, **between 20 and 75** - **Favourable transits** are required for a big place in life
- **B07-R027**
  **B07-R027 — Prime Ministership (K.N. Rao's parameters, published in JOA 1997)** *(p.213)*: - **The dasha of the 5th and 9th lords gives ministership.** - **The dasha or antardasha of the 4th or 10th lord — or of a planet associated with the 4th house / 4th lord — can give "the throne."** - **Transit of Saturn and Jupiter mainly on the 5th or 9th house or its lord.**

Note the **number of yogas** present (Nabhasa, Sun-based, Moon-based) 5. Strength and **PAC of the kendra lords (1, 4, 7, 10)** 6. Strength and **PAC of the trikona lords (1, 5, 9)** 7. Strength and **PAC of the dusthana lords (6, 8, 12)** 8. Strength of the **dhana lords (2nd and 11th)** 9. Strength and **PAC of the 3rd lord** (upachaya: 3, 6, 10, 11)

## B08 (48)

- **B08-R001**
  **B08-R001 — Natural classification.** Malefics: **Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu, and the waning Moon from the 7th day**. Benefics: **Jupiter, Venus, Mercury (when with benefics), and the waxing Moon from the 7th day**. *(p.27)*
- **B08-R002**
  **B08-R002 — The functional rules, by lordship** *(pp.27–28)*: 1. **Lagna lord is benefic** — beneficence varying with the other sign it owns; *not applicable to Sun and Moon, which own a single sign*. 2. **Kendra lords are benefic.** 3. **Trikona lords are benefic.** 4. A planet owning **both a kendra and a trikona becomes a Yogakaraka**. 5. Lords of the **3rd, 6th and 11th are malefic**. 6. The **8th lord is malefic — but its maleficence is not total if it also owns the Lagna, a trikona or a kendra**. 7. Lords of the **2nd and 12th are of dual nature** — good or bad by position; **they can give Rajayoga results in the dasha or antardasha of yogakaraka planets**. 8. A planet owning a **kendra and the 3rd/6th/11th becomes neutral**. 9. A planet owning a **trikona and the 3rd/6th/11th becomes benefic**. 10. A planet **both of whose signs fall in the 3rd, 6th, 8th or 11th becomes quite negative** for the native.
- **B08-R003**
  **B08-R003 — Always check Shadbala and directional strength** for any planet so classified; **any planet owning the Lagna is mostly benefic and very little malefic**. *(p.29)*
- **B08-R004**
  **B08-R004 — Nodes as yogakarakas.** **Rahu and Ketu in kendras or trikonas become yogakarakas; if aspected by or associated with other yogakarakas in those houses, they confer Rajayogas.** *(p.29)*
- **B08-R005**
  **B08-R005 — Retrogression.** **Natural benefics retrograde promote prosperity, success and happiness; natural malefics retrograde indicate delays and obstacles.** **The house occupied by a retrograde planet is affected.** A **retrograde planet conjunct the Sun causes confrontation with higher authorities during its dasha.** *(pp.29–30)*
- **B08-R006**
  **B08-R006 — Combustion orbs** (either side of the Sun): **Moon 12° · Mars 17° · Mercury 14° · Venus 10° · Jupiter 11° · Saturn 15°**; **any planet within 4° is in deep combustion**. A combust planet comes under the Sun's influence and **its own benefic or malefic effect tends to reduce in both cases**. *(p.30)*
- **B08-R008**
  **B08-R008 — Badhaka.** Badhakas **indicate obstacles; they do not deny an event, but certainly cause impediments. Planets in badhaka houses also become badhakas.** **Movable → 11th lord · Fixed → 9th lord · Dual → 7th lord.** By sign: Aries–Saturn, Taurus–Saturn, Gemini–Jupiter, Cancer–Venus, Leo–Mars, Virgo–Jupiter, Libra–Sun, Scorpio–Moon, Sagittarius–Mercury, Capricorn–Mars, Aquarius–Venus, Pisces–Mercury. *(pp.30–31)*
- **B08-R009**
  **B08-R009 — The separative planets.** **Sun, Saturn, Mars and Rahu** give **separation from the events of the houses they influence** unless balanced by benefics. **Sun is the least malefic** — separative only when under another malefic by aspect/association, or through a malefic dispositor, or when a malefic sits in Leo. *(p.32)*
- **B08-R010**
  **B08-R010 — *Kendriya* effect (the 10th-from-a-planet rule).** Every planet **remains under the influence of whatever occupies the 10th from itself** (kendra-from-kendra). Therefore **the positive or negative effects during a planet's antardasha depend on the nature of the planets placed in the 10th from it.** *(p.32)*
- **B08-R011**
  **B08-R011 — The nodes.** Rahu and Ketu **aspect the 5th, 7th and 9th**; unassociated, they give **the results of Saturn and Mars respectively**, plus the effects of the house lord they occupy, and **of their dispositor**. They carry the effect of planets conjoining/aspecting them **into their own aspect value**; and **their dispositors carry Rahu/Ketu's effects into their aspects — visible in the MD and AD of those dispositors.** *(pp.32–33)*
- **B08-R012**
  **B08-R012 — A house lord inherits from its occupants**; influenced by Saturn, Mars etc., such a lord **may cause separation from that house's events and from the houses it aspects.** *(p.33)*
- **B08-R013**
  **B08-R013 — Tattva of houses, and its variability.** 1/5/9 = **Agni** · 2/6/10 = **Earth** · 3/7/11 = **Air** · 4/8/12 = **Water**. But the natural quality varies: **the Sun, fiery, acts as a watery planet placed in the 4th.** Likewise **Jupiter becomes inauspicious if his signs are occupied by Mars and Saturn, and gives inauspicious results in his MD/AD despite being a natural benefic.** *(p.33)*
- **B08-R014**
  **B08-R014 — Two-sign owners give "two-in-one" results.** Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn own two signs — **consider the functional dasha indications from both points of view**. **Sun and Moon own one house each, hence their results as house-lord in various houses can be more relied upon.** *(p.33)*
- **B08-R015**
  **B08-R015 — A good planet may still fail.** Consider the position and effects properly: **even a good benefic may not give good things in its period if it is ill-affected by sign or position, afflicted by a majority of malefics, or badly placed in the relevant divisional chart (D-9, D-7, D-10).** *(pp.33–34)*

  **B08-R016 — Judge the AD lord exactly as you judged the MD lord — *plus* its relation to the MD lord.** Natural friendship/enmity is not enough: **the functional nature arising from placement in the horoscope must be looked at carefully. Friendly and benefic planets may not give positive effects if they are functionally weak** by placement, aspect or conjunction. *(p.34)*

  **B08-R017 — Karaka intensification by transit.** *"The effects of the ruling or karaka planets are intensified during these dashas if they are transiting through the signs occupied by them, or the signs over which they rule."* *(p.34)*

  **B08-R018 — Pratyantar is judged to the AD exactly as the AD is judged to the MD.** *"At the outset a negative-looking effect may actually be positive, or vice versa, which should be carefully found out."* *(p.34)*

  **B08-R020 — The AD lord's sign, read as a house from the Lagna, colours the sub-period.** If the MD is Sun's and the AD Venus's, and **Venus occupies Aquarius, the effects appear in 11th-house matters; if Venus occupies Libra, in 7th-house affairs.** *(p.35)*

Partial aspects: all planets except Saturn have a **quarter aspect on the 3rd and 10th**; all except Jupiter a **half aspect on the 5th and 9th**; all except Mars a **three-quarter aspect on the 4th and 8th**. *(pp.35–36)*

  **B08-R026 — In any mahadasha, it is the antardasha lord that is more influencing and effective during its own period.** *(p.36)*

The effects pertaining to the house which happens to be their Moolatrikona sign shall be more predominant."* Moolatrikona signs: **Sun–Leo, Moon–Taurus, Mars–Aries, Mercury–Virgo, Jupiter–Sagittarius, Venus–Libra, Saturn–Aquarius, Rahu–Cancer, Ketu–Gemini.** *(p.42)*

- **B08-R034**
  **B08-R034 — What a dasha lord actually delivers — the five sources.** A dasha lord gives results according to **(i) its lordship**, **(ii) the planets influencing it by conjunction, aspect and placement**, **(iii) the house it occupies**, **(iv) its dispositor**, and **(v) the natural signification and house-lordship of any planet aspecting or conjoining it**. *(pp.37–38)*
- **B08-R035**
  **B08-R035 — Occupancy modifies house significations.** The significations of houses **occupied by benefics are increased and by malefics reduced**. **Dasha lords aspecting their own houses increase those houses' significations**, as do planets in **mutual reception**. *(pp.37–38)*

  **B08-R036 — The 5th lord is privileged.** **The combination of the MD or AD of the 9th or 10th lord with that of the 5th lord is highly auspicious.** **Any planet combined with the 5th lord — and the aspect of the 5th lord — indicates auspicious results during that dasha.** *(p.39)*

  **B08-R037 — The three permanent benefics.** *"Lagna lord is to be treated as most benefic for every Lagna even if it owns some other negative houses. Similarly for the 5th and 9th houses."* *(p.39)*

  **B08-R038 — Trikona lordship makes no distinction between natural benefics and malefics.** *"Benefics or malefics as the 5th lord are equally good."* And **Sun as 5th lord for Aries, Moon as 5th lord for Pisces, own no other house and are therefore quite beneficial.** *(p.88)*

  **B08-R039 — The trika-lord / kendra-trikona-lord swap.** *"Whenever the lords of trika houses (6, 8, 12) join a kendra lord (1, 4, 7, 10) or trikona lord (1, 5, 9), the dasha or antardasha of the trika lord becomes auspicious, and the dasha or antardasha of the kendra or trikona lord becomes inauspicious or just ordinary."* *(pp.92–93)*

  **B08-R040 — Judge the period lord through its nakshatra lord.** Check **which nakshatra the MD/AD lord occupies, and where that nakshatra's lord is placed** — the effects of that nakshatra lord's house then appear within the period. *(p.42, p.50)*
- **B08-R041**
  **B08-R041 — The nakshatra lord should sit in a propitious tara from the Janma Nakshatra** — **Sampat (2nd), Kshema (4th), Sadhaka (6th), Mitra (8th) or Ati-Mitra (9th)**. *(p.43)*

  **B08-R042 — Read every dasha from all three reference points (per Parashara): Sun, Moon and Lagna.** *(p.43)*
- **B08-R043**
  **B08-R043 — The three references have different tempos.** *"Results experienced due to the **Sun last long and change slowly**; due to the **Moon last shorter and change fast**; due to the **Lagna change even faster**."* *(p.43)*
- **B08-R044**

## B13 (19)

  **B23-R002 — The birth-time error.** Three classical positions on what counts as the moment of birth — **the first cry**, **the severing of the physical link with the mother**, or **the emergence of the head**. *"Which one should we consider? If one is correct then the other two are wrong. **Using the same ayanamsha, the balance of the period of a planet differs as per different schools.**"* *(p.2)*
- **B23-R003**
  **B23-R003 — The author's answer.** *"In this book **I may not be able to tell you the exact amount of ayanamsha, but I can assure you that by using my method of synchronization you can tell the exact beginning and end of a period**."* *(p.1)* — i.e. **the method sidesteps the ayanamsha question entirely by fitting the dasha to observed events.**
- **B23-R008**
  **B23-R008 — Moon Base, not Sun or Ascendant base.** The author tested **Ashtottari, Yogini, Vimshottari and Tribhagi on Moon base, Sun base and Ascendant base.** Findings *(pp.3–4)*: - **Ashtottari on Moon base — "I found it did not work."** - **Ashtottari on Sun base — "unsatisfactory."** - **Ashtottari on Ascendant base — "without any worthwhile results."** - **Yogini — "I was dissatisfied."** - **Vimshottari on Sun base and Ascendant base — "here too I was not satisfied."** - ***"Finally I worked on Vimshottari Dasha and Tribhagi Dasha taking MOON BASE, and found that it gave me satisfactory results."***
- **B23-R009**
  **B23-R009 — Why the Ascendant chart carries the detail, and the Moon chart does not.** *"The **Ascendant changes approximately after two hours**; the **Moon Ascendant lasts for 2½ days**. That is why the details of a native's events are recollected **from the Ascendant chart only** — **all those born over 2½ days cannot have the same fate.**"* *(p.20)* > **Note the apparent tension with R008 and reconcile them as the author does in practice: the *dasha* is computed on Moon base; the *event detail* is read from the Ascendant chart, with the Moon-Ascendant chart used as the second reference.**
- **B23-R010**
  **B23-R010 — The five "places."** The author numbers the levels: **Mahadasha = 1st place · Antardasha = 2nd · Pratyantar = 3rd · Sookshma = 4th · Prana = 5th.** *(p.5)*
- **B23-R011**
  **B23-R011 — Ignore the first place.** ***"Do not give much importance to the first place, because a planet in first place runs for many years — from 6 years of Sun to 20 of Venus. The entire period of first place cannot go in the same manner. As a whole, a first-place period cannot be totally good or bad. Therefore give importance to the SECOND, THIRD, FOURTH and FIFTH places."*** *(pp.5, 10)*
- **B23-R015**
  **B23-R015 — Computing the 4th and 5th places.** **Divide the number of days in the third place by 120 → a constant. Multiply that constant by the Vimshottari years of each planet → that planet's duration in days at the next level.** Repeat downward for the 5th, 6th, 7th places. *(Worked: a third place of 144 days ÷ 120 = 1.2; × 6 = Sun's share; × 10 = 12 days for the Moon.)* *(pp.5–6)*
- **B23-R016**
  **B23-R016 — Rule 1: Exchange (parivartana) — substitution runs BOTH ways.** *"If planet A is in the sign of B and planet B is in the sign of A, this is an **Exchange**. While synchronizing, **A can replace B and B can replace A**."* *(p.6)*
- **B23-R017**
  **B23-R017 — Rule 2: Aspect Exchange — also BOTH ways.** *"If A aspects B's sign and B aspects A's sign, this is **Aspect Exchange**. **In this case too, A can replace B and B can replace A.**"* *(p.7)*
- **B23-R018**
  **B23-R018 — Rule 3: A planet in another planet's nakshatra — substitution is ONE-WAY.** *"Suppose Mars is in the constellation of Saturn. **Mars can replace Saturn, but Saturn cannot replace Mars**"* — meaning **Mars can act as Mars and also as Saturn.** *(p.7)*
- **B23-R019**
  **B23-R019 — Rule 4: A planet in another planet's sign — also ONE-WAY.** *"Mars situated in the house of Mercury: **Mars can replace Mercury, but Mercury cannot replace Mars.**"* The same one-way rule applies through **aspect**: a planet aspecting another's sign may replace that other, but not conversely. *(pp.7–8)*
- **B23-R021**
  **B23-R021 — The author's astronomical note on the nodes.** Every planet has nodes where its orbit crosses the ecliptic — Mercury inclined 7°, Venus 3°24′, Mars 1°51′, Jupiter 1°19′, Saturn likewise. *"So why consider the nodes of the Moon only? **Because it is the nearest, and Rahu/Ketu of the Moon return to their original position after 18 years. Other planets' nodes take hundreds of years — which cannot be noticed in our life span.**"* *(p.9)*
- **B23-R023**
  **B23-R023 — The tolerance thresholds — the acceptance test.** *(pp.13–14)* - Compute the predicted date from the combination. **If the difference from the real date exceeds four or five months (±) in either Vimshottari or Tribhagi, DISREGARD that fit — because a difference of that size would normally change the Ascendant.** - Order of attempts: **Ascendant chart on Vimshottari → Ascendant chart on Tribhagi (tolerance now 3–4 months) → Moon Ascendant chart on Vimshottari → Moon Ascendant chart on Tribhagi.** - ***"If the difference is 15 days, 1 month or 1½ months, you can make the adjustment."***
- **B23-R024**
  **B23-R024 — Tolerance scales with the balance-at-birth lord.** *(p.14)* - **Balance of a long-period lord — Venus (20), Saturn (19), Rahu (18), Jupiter (16):** the acceptable difference is **under 2–3 months in Vimshottari, under 1–2 months in Tribhagi.** - **Balance of a short-period lord — Sun (6), Ketu (7), Mars (7), Moon (10):** the difference **must not exceed 1–2 months in Vimshottari, and 15 days to 1½ months in Tribhagi.**

## B24 (14)

- **B24-R003**
  **B24-R003 — House 9 is the ignition point — "the beginning of karma."** *"**The fortunate period of the native will begin — positive or negative — when the planet in this house becomes active.** The house is viewed as a great ocean."* If house 9 is **inactive, it can be activated through house 2 — provided houses 3 and 5 are vacant.** *(p.35–36)*
- **B24-R006**
  **B24-R006 — Delayed activation from houses 6 and 8.** *"**Mercury and Ketu will not give good outcomes until the age of the planet in house 6 or 8.**"* *(p.34)*
- **B24-R008**
  **B24-R008 — House 7 — Venus's results cannot be remedied.** House 7 is where the native *"gets ground between the earth and the sky for food"* — Venus and Mercury being its natural significators, Venus its lord. **Sun, Moon and Rahu give results according to the placement of Venus.** And the hard rule: ***"Bad outcomes due to the placement of Venus CANNOT be improved or mitigated by remedies. These outcomes will happen and the remedies will all go waste."*** *(p.35)* > **Contrast: "Mercury, Saturn and Ketu will give their results at different periods. If those results are bad, we may attempt remedial measures and they will be fixed or improved."** — i.e. **Lal Kitab holds that some periods are remediable and some are not, and names which.**
- **B24-R009**
  **B24-R009 — Gender of planets and of afflicted relatives.** *"Normally the **male planets affect male relations** and the **female planets affect the female ones**. In house 7, if the planets are more than two in number, **only the male relatives are affected**."* **Male planets: Sun, Mars, Jupiter. Female: Moon, Venus.** *(p.35)*
- **B24-R011**
  **B24-R011 — The 2-4-8 triad, and the Moon's rescuing power.** *"House 8 has its impact on house 6. If either house is afflicted the outcomes are bad for both — in such cases **the planets in the 12th should also be judged**… **The Moon is the most powerful planet for the affairs of the 8th house. The Moon, by strength of his position, can fix the evil of the 12th house also.** The cause of the malefic nature of the 8th house cannot but be house 4 through house 2. **Therefore houses 2, 4 and 8 should be judged simultaneously.**"* And: **planets in house 8, if hostile to the planets in houses 2 and 11, "will strike at the most vulnerable point."** *(p.35)*
- **B24-R012**
  **B24-R012 — House 9 protections.** *"**If Sun or Moon occupy house 9, there will be no bad effects of even Rahu and Ketu.** House No. 9 is the seat of Jupiter."* **Planets in house 5 aspect the planets in house 9.** *(p.36)*
- **B24-R013**
  **B24-R013 — House 10 depends on house 4 and vice versa.** *"**If house 10 is vacant, the planets in house 4 cannot give good outcomes.** Saturn is the natural significator of the house [10]."* *(p.36)*
- **B24-R017**
  **B24-R017 — House 11 modified by house 12.** *"The planet in house 11, its situation and position, will give a glimpse of the worldly status of the native. **This can be modified by the condition of house No. 12.**"* *(p.36)*
- **B24-R019**
  **B24-R019 — The Saturn exception on aspects.** *"Although the planets in house 6 are aspected by the planets in house 2, **the position would be reversed in the case of Saturn, who will aspect house 2 from the 6th**."* *(p.35)*
- **B24-R020**
  **B24-R020 — Jupiter's requirement in the annual progressed chart.** *"Jupiter ought to be benefic **by his situation and position in the yearly progressed charts**."* *(p.33)* — i.e. **Lal Kitab requires the annual progressed chart, not only the natal, before pronouncing on Jupiter.**
- **B24-R021**
  **B24-R021 — Structure.** The section *"Effects of Maha Dasha & Antar Dasha"* (from p.37) gives, for each of the nine lords: **"General Understandings"** — the general effects of that Mahadasha — followed by **"Specific Understandings"** — *"interpretations in view of the condition of the planet in the birth chart and divisional charts"* — and then **all nine Antardashas within it.** This is standard BPHS-derived material.
- **B24-R022**
  **B24-R022 — The general/specific split is the important structural rule.** Each Mahadasha is read twice: once for its **general character** (what the lord does by nature), and once **as modified by that planet's condition in the birth chart and the divisional charts.** *(p.37)*
- **B24-R029**
  **B24-R029 — The two-tier remediability rule — the most useful thing in the book.** Per **R008**: **bad results arising from Venus's placement in house 7 cannot be remedied at all — "the remedies will all go waste." But bad results from Mercury, Saturn and Ketu at their respective periods CAN be fixed or improved by remedial measures.** *(p.35)* > **Before prescribing for a difficult period, establish which planet is actually the source — because Lal Kitab holds that not every source is remediable.**
- **B24-R031**
  **B24-R031 — The author's stated position.** *"Astrology in a broader sense represents the karma of the native. It also indicates certain remedies."* The seven layers of existence — **body, breath, mind, intellect, memory, ego, self**. *"As long as one identifies with the first six layers, the laws of karma act. **Once one goes beyond the sixth — ego — the true spirit is beyond any karma, and beyond any astrology.**"* Hence, to the question of whether astrology binds: *"the answer is both yes and no."* *(Preface)*
