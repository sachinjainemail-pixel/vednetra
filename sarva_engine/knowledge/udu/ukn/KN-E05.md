- **B01-R043** · B01 · keyword match
  **B01-R043 — The ascending grades of yoga-giver** (each stronger than the last): - **Karta** — lord of / placed with / aspecting **Lagna, HL or GL**. Contact with Lagna → self-development; with HL → financial improvement; with GL → improvement in power, authority, status. - **Kevala** — the **subhapati** (Moon-sign lord) becomes Karta; gives great material prospects. - **Yogada** — a planet **simultaneously** aspects/conjoins/owns **Lagna *and* HL** (→ *Dhana* yogada) **or Lagna *and* GL** (→ *Raja* yogada). Ownership of the **7th from** these signs also counts. - **Kevala Yogada** — the subhapati becomes a Yogada. - **Maha Yogada** — a planet simultaneously aspects/occupies/owns **Lagna, HL *and* GL** (or the 7ths from them). **In its dasa it bestows excellent prosperity, in the antar of an auspicious planet related to it by aspect, conjunction or exchange.** - **Kevala Maha Yogada** — the subhapati becomes Mahayogada. **In its dasa the native can rise from rags to riches.** *(p.86)*
