  **B01-R021 — Graha dasa vs. rasi dasa — what each actually shows.** A **Naisargika Graha Dasa shows what we *are*** during that period (we take on the qualities of the graha); a **Naisargika Rasi Dasa shows what we *get*** — the field, circumstance and environment handed to us. Penury or riches, disease or health, is what the world gives (rasi); how one acts within it is what one is (graha). *(p.83)*
