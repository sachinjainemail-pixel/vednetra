## Ashvini — timing (24)

  **IF** A planet in the nakshatra of its enemy — e.g. Saturn in Mars's nakshatra Mrigashira
  **THEN** Negative outcomes; events take the form of clashes between the significations of the two enemy planets.
- **ASH-0432** · nak Mrigashira · ASH p145 · ~/stated
  **IF** Mrigashira nakshatra in the chart
  **THEN** Activates in the 4th, 8th, 17th, 25th, 34th, 46th, 50th, 65th, 73rd and 79th years of age.
- **AVA-2-1017** · nak Mrigashira · AVA-2 p116 · -/stated
