- **B01-R020** · B01 · keyword match
  **B01-R020 — Adjust the per-sign years to the native's longevity kakshya.** Purnaayu 108 splits into three khandas — **alpaayu 0–36, madhyaayu 36–72, purnaayu 72–108** — each further split into short/middle/long kakshya. Then: - longevity in the **short** kakshya → movable sign **7** years, fixed **8**, dual **9** (as in **Sthira Dasa**); - **middle** kakshya → **9** years per sign (as in **Shoola Dasa**); - **long** kakshya → **10** years per sign (totalling the 120-year maximum of Kali Yuga). *(p.83)*
