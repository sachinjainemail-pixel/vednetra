## How to read a UDU rule line

Example. `- **AVA-2-0092** · `marriage` · nak Ashvini, pada 2, planet Moon · AVA-2 p21 · +/stated` followed by an IF line and a THEN line. The ID is the citation tag. The backtick field is the life event. Then the placement, the source book and page, then polarity and confidence. Polarity + is benefic, - is malefic, ~ is mixed. Confidence is classical 1.2, stated 1.0, inferred 0.7. Specificity weight is pada 1.5, planet in nakshatra 1.3, nakshatra 1.0, general 0.6. Those three multiply to give the Kernel 0 weight.

## Warnings carried from the corpus

1. A zero or a thin count is a hole in the books and never an astrological silence. 25 of the 567 nakshatra by category cells are empty.
2. `travel` at 175 rules, `matching` at 191, `property` at 194 and `mundane` at 61 are the thinnest categories. Check `UDU-REF-coverage.md` before reading a low count as a denial.
3. Five KN books are defective. B06 badhaka table page 58, B10 four Yogini lords wrong, B15 tara table and balance formula, B18 Ashtottari balance, B24 machine paraphrased and never quoted.
4. ASH carries no genuine Venus in Dhanishta content. NAT has no Ashlesha Ayurveda section.
5. A UDU rule may never be cited for a timing claim. A KN rule may never be cited for a nakshatra content claim.
