## Ashvini — muhurta (19)

- **ASH-0176** · nak Ashvini · ASH p78 · +/stated

  **THEN** Favourable for commencing physical exercise, art and craft, healing, rejuvenation, medical or occult study, helping others, health treatment, manufacturing, construction, installing deities, renovation, name change, trading, money lending, sacred thread ceremony and laying a project foundation.
