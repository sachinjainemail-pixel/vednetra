# Excluded outcomes

Owner rule. The engine does not decide medical outcomes, death, pregnancy outcome or speculative gain.
These topics return NOT DECIDED in every report.

The owner's supplement on these topics is reference text only. It includes classical rules, such as the
22nd drekkana and Jupiter transit rules for death, and rules for miscarriage and trading. These rules are not
encoded, because the owner's own note says these outcomes cannot be measured reliably from a chart.

Also not encoded. Divisional charts beyond D1 and D9, and Jaimini points such as upapada and darakaraka.
